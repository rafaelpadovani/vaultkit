Overview

VaultKit is an app that stores your photos, videos, documents and notes in encrypted vaults on your iPhone. Each vault opens with a pattern you draw, and every pattern opens its own separate vault. If you do not agree, please do not use VaultKit.

You Hold the Only Keys

Your vaults are encrypted with keys derived from your pattern and a secret kept on your device. VaultKit has no accounts and no servers, and we never have these keys. We cannot open, reset or recover a vault for you.

Each vault has a recovery phrase. If you forget a vault's pattern and lose its recovery phrase, that vault and everything in it cannot be recovered by anyone. Keep your recovery phrases somewhere safe and never share them.

Features That Erase Data

Some features permanently destroy data by design:

  • Duress vault. When the pattern of a vault set up as a duress vault is drawn, every other vault on the device is erased without warning, including their recovery phrases.
  • Delete this vault erases one vault and everything in it.
  • Erase everything destroys every vault on the device and the secret their keys depend on.

Erased data cannot be restored by us or by anyone else. You use these features at your own risk.

Keep Your Own Copies

VaultKit keeps your files on your iPhone. It is not a backup service. If your iPhone is lost, damaged or reset without a device backup, the vaults on it are gone. Keep separate copies of anything you cannot afford to lose.
